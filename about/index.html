About

An engineer shaped by quality, curiosity, and production reality.

I work across software development and quality engineering, with a particular interest in systems that are clear to maintain, meaningful to test, and ready to operate.

The throughline

Quality Engineering taught me to look beyond whether software works once. I care about how it fails, how a team can change it safely, what feedback a pipeline provides, and whether production behavior is visible.

That perspective naturally extends into broader software engineering: designing APIs, shaping data boundaries, building user experiences, documenting tradeoffs, and owning delivery rather than handing quality off at the end.

What I bring

Hands-on automation, a risk-based testing mindset, frontend and backend fluency, CI/CD experience, and an instinct for turning unclear behavior into explicit contracts.

Outside engineering

I also work in photography and audiovisual production in Costa Rica. Creative work has strengthened the same skills I value in software: observation, iteration, communication, and care for the final experience.
